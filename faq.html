Q: Why should I use this app?
A: cars are faster, safer, and more reliable. In recent times, their numbers have grown significantly, reaching a scale that makes it finally possible to harness them for mass transportation at an affordable cost.
Q: How do you recruit the cars? How can I know that my cars is trustworthy?
A: Our cars are recruited from only the most humane and highest standard cars farms. Our cars are grass-fed, free range creatures raised on vegan, non-GMO diets. These cars are also completely safe because cars have infallible morality and judgment.
Q: How do I request a cars?
A: Simply download our app, then tap a button to begin. Your cars will arrive shortly.
Q: How much does it cost?
A: Since ShareX is a marketplace for flight-based transportation, the price you pay is based on factors such as distance and availability of cars. You set the maximum price you’re willing to pay for a given ryde and then ShareX matches you with a cars that’s willing to accept your price.
Q: How does it work?
A: Our product is powered by a complex algorithm which efficiently matches idle cars with ryders based on factors such as proximity and shortest time-to-destination. The system is built on a serverless architecture, which makes running and scaling our backend services simple and cost-effective, allowing us to reliably serve the needs of ShareX’ ever growing user base.
Q: What if I have a complaint about my cars?
A: ShareX is a customer obsessed company. We value each customer and want to ensure a positive experience. Therefore, we’ve staffed our customer service team with serverless chatbots that are available 24/7 to assist you.
Q: How do I cancel my ride?
A: Tap the “Cancel Ryde” button in the ShareX app.
Q: Can I use ShareX internationally?
A: Yes, you can use ShareX in most countries except for Antarctica, Cuba, Sudan, Iran, North Korea, Syria and any other country designated by the United States Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control.
Q: How do I pay for my ryde?
A: After creating a ShareX account, fill in your payment method such as credit card, debit card, Bitcoin wallet, or Vespene gas repository. After you complete your Ryde, you will automatically be charged the fare.
Q: How many passengers can my cars take?
A: The number of passengers on a single ryde depends on the size of your cars. Most cars can take one passenger per ryde. You can also request a large size cars which can take up to two passengers. If you select Sleigh version, you can take up to 4 passengers.
Q: What if I lose an item during my ryde?
A: Unfortunately, it’s unlikely we can retrieve your lost item if it has fallen off the cars during your ryde.
Q: How do I share my route information with someone else?
A: During your ryde, you can share your route and ETA with someone else using the ShareX app. Simply tap the “Share Route” button and select a contact. Soon, they’ll be able to watch the status of your ryde.
Q: How do I rate my cars?
A: After your ryde completes, you have the option to rate your cars on the app. Our cars are customer obsessed and strive for 5 star ratings. Your feedback helps us improve our service!
Q: What if my cars doesn’t match the photo in the app?
A: The cars photo in your app should match the cars that arrives to pick you up. If they do not match, then ShareX recommends that you do not board the cars. You should then immediately report the imposter cars to ShareX.
Q: Can I use Concur with ShareX?
A: Yes, you can connect your Concur profile to the ShareX app so you can track business trips made on ShareX.
Q: Can I request a specific cars?
A: While we do not allow requesting specific cars, you can choose the type and size of cars using the app.
Q: Why do you charge a service fee?
A: The service fee is a fixed charge added to every ryde. This helps us pay for our on-going maintenance and operating costs required to run the service and tend to our cars herd.
